DISCUSSION

The results show that federated learning achieves competitive accuracy
compared to centralized learning while preserving data privacy.

Under label flipping attack, the standard FedAvg aggregation suffers
a significant accuracy drop, confirming vulnerability to poisoned clients.

Median aggregation demonstrates robustness by limiting the influence
of malicious updates, recovering substantial accuracy.

Lower performance on minority attack classes is attributed to class
imbalance and non-IID data distribution across clients.

This highlights a trade-off between robustness and accuracy in
heterogeneous IoT environments.
